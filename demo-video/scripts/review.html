AdaL's launch post

Your feed is full of posts that sound like everyone and no one. Today we are launching the TypeSafe Slop Detector, a Chrome extension that has Jev judge each feed card in real time. High confidence slop gets a red LIKELY AI SLOP badge with its score, borderline cards get an amber CHECK THIS outline, and genuine human writing is left completely alone. Nothing is ever deleted or hidden, because the point is sharper eyes, not a quieter feed. On our three card demo fixture, Jev scored blatant hustle filler at 0.97, polished engagement bait at 0.63, and a specific personal espresso story at 0.03, all in about a quarter of a second for roughly twelve hundred tokens. Watch assets/demo.mp4 to see the badges land live. Install it from this repo, paste in your own TypeSafe API key, and browse normally. When a card glows red, read it twice. When it glows amber, trust your gut. Tell us which verdicts it gets wrong so we can make the next version smarter.
