Slop Detector

Marks likely AI slop in your X feed as you scroll

Every post is judged in real time by a confidence model and marked in place. You keep scrolling, you keep seeing everything, and the likely slop now announces itself.

Three bands, no verdicts

A model that answers one question per post: how likely is this low-effort AI generated slop, from 0 to 100. The number paints the card. You make the call.

Getting it

The extension is a plain unpacked folder. It talks to a small relay on your own machine, which is where the model key lives, so nothing sensitive ships inside the extension.

  1. 01
    Run npm run relay in the repo, with TYPESAFE_API_KEY set in your environment.

    The relay listens on port 8787 and forwards one score question at a time.

  2. 02
    Load the extension/ folder as an unpacked extension.

    Chrome or Edge, developer mode on, then open x.com.

  3. 03
    Scroll.

    Cards are judged as they enter the viewport, one question each, marked in place.

What it will not do

  • 01
    Hide, collapse, reorder, blur or filter anything. The overlays are additive, and a failed judgment changes nothing.
  • 02
    Call a post AI generated. It reports a confidence that something is likely slop, and the wording keeps to that.
  • 03
    Ask a follow-up question about a post. One card, one question, one score.
