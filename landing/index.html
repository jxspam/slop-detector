Slop Detector

Marks likely AI slop in your feed as you scroll.

A Chrome MV3 extension powered by TypeSafe Jev System One decisions. Evaluates posts in real time without obscuring, hiding, or deleting content.

Install Extension

Calibrated confidence bands

Every post goes through TypeSafe Jev as a single score question. Decisions land across three clear bands instead of binary blacklists.

01

Score 80 and above

Receives a compact red pill badge showing LIKELY AI SLOP with the exact confidence percentage.

02

Score 40 to 79

Receives an amber outline around the card. Prompts the reader to inspect phrasing without issuing an accusation.

03

Score under 40

Remains completely untouched. No badge, no outline, no visual noise.

Non-destructive design

Unlike filters that collapse or remove tweets, Slop Detector is purely additive. Your timeline remains whole. Failed API calls leave cards unchanged.

Run locally

Start the local relay server, load the unpacked extension into Edge or Chrome, and browse your feed.

git clone https://github.com/jxspam/slop-detector.git cd slop-detector export TYPESAFE_API_KEY="your_api_key" node relay/server.js
